How it works

Three steps, and a stamp.

  1. 01

    Drop in your documents

    PDFs (page-level citations), text, Markdown, HTML or a URL, into named collections. Vouch detects each document's language and indexes it for meaning and keywords.

  2. 02

    Ask by voice or text

    Tap the mic and speak. The browser can transcribe with no key; Sarvam handles Indian languages and Whisper the rest. Typing works too, in any script.

  3. 03

    Get the quote, or an honest no

    The answer is copied from the best passage and stamped with its source. Tap a citation to see the passage with the quote highlighted. If nothing answers it, you get "not in your documents".

Languages

Speak the way you speak.

Who it's for

For people who don't type in English, and can't afford a confident wrong answer.

Public service desks

Ration cards, water bills, permits. Callers and walk-ins ask in their own language; staff and kiosks answer from the official circulars.

Field workers

Community health workers and extension officers ask a phone out loud and get the protocol sentence, with the page it came from.

Institutions

Universities, councils and HR teams turn handbooks and regulations into a desk that knows when to say "ask a person".
